Frame By Frame

September 2026

About Game

Frame By Frame is a 2D puzzle game. The player is a night guard at an art museum where two paintings, American Gothic and Nighthawks, have lost their frames. The guard steps into each painting's world to recover its frame and find out what went wrong.


➤ Entry for SGDA Fall '26 Jam. Developed in 10 days for the theme "Beyond the Border."

➤ Role: Lead Artist, Environment Art, Character Art

Lead Artist

I created the game's visual assets in Aseprite.

  • Designed the museum and the worlds inside both paintings.
  • Drew the props and interactive objects the player uses to solve puzzles.
  • Drew some of the NPC sprites, working alongside a second character artist.

Art Style

Like A Question of Taste, the game is top-down on a 32 × 32 grid with a minimal palette. The challenge was to adapt two well-known paintings into small, limited-color pixel environments that still read as the originals.
