A Historical Typing Simulator

A U.S. history simulation game. You play as a typist for the Office of Naval Intelligence, c. 1890-1929.

Transcribe documents, learn about the early imperial age of America, and question everything.


➤ Developed for my high school AP Capstone Research project.

➤ Role: Solo Developer

About The Project

This game was developed to explore the intersection of early American imperialism and interactive storytelling. As a solo developer, I handled the programming in Unity, as well as the historical research required to accurately simulate documents from the 1890s to 1929.
