A Historical Typing Simulator

A U.S. history simulation game. You play as a typist for the Office of Naval Intelligence, c. 1890-1929. Transcribe documents, learn about the early imperial age of America, and question everything.


➤ This is for my high school senior AP Capstone Research, measuring the difference in information retention between APUSH students who play the game v. students who traditionally read primary documents.
